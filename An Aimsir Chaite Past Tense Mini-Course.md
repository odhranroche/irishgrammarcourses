## Course overview

Seventeen lessons of 5–10 minutes each take you from the first letter of a regular verb to reporting what someone said with an irregular one. Each lesson teaches one small rule, shows it in short model sentences from a TEG B1 course book, then drills it in sets of five until it is automatic.

The whole past tense rests on two ideas:

1. **The first letter of the root decides the statement form.** Consonant → séimhiú (*chuir*); l, n, r, sc, sm, sp, st → no change (*léigh*); vowel → *d'* (*d'ól*); f → *d'fh* (*d'fhág*).
2. **Two families of little words go in front of the verb.** Regular verbs and five irregulars take the *-r* words. The other six irregulars take the older words with a special "dependent" stem.

|  | The *-r* family + séimhiú | The older family + dependent stem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Used with | All regular verbs + *beir, clois, ith, tabhair, tar* | *bí, téigh, déan, feic, faigh, abair* |
| Question | *ar* — *Ar chuir tú?* | *an* + urú — *An ndeachaigh tú?* |
| No | *níor* — *Níor chuir.* | *ní* + séimhiú — *Ní dheachaigh.* |
| That | *gur* — *Dúirt sé gur chuir sé é.* | *go* + urú — *Dúirt sé go ndeachaigh sé.* |
| That… not | *nár* — *nár chuir* | *nach* + urú — *nach ndeachaigh* |
| Where? | *cár* — *Cár fhan tú?* | *cá* + urú — *Cá ndeachaigh tú?* |
| If not | *murar* — *Murar fhreastail…* | *mura* + urú — *mura raibh* |

## How every lesson runs

Every lesson has the same seven steps, and each step goes out as its own message so feedback comes after every five answers.

1. **Recall (1 min).** Three quick items from earlier lessons, so old rules stay warm. Lesson 1 has a short Starter instead, since nothing comes before it.
2. **Model sentences (1 min).** Short source sentences with the target forms in bold. Read them aloud once. Unconnected sentences are listed one by one; a connected story is set as a paragraph.
3. **The rule (1 min).** One small table. Nothing else is new.
4. **Set A — Notice (5 items).** Spot or build the form with the rule in front of you.
5. **Set B — Apply (5 items).** Use the rule in sentences, usually as question / yes / no.
6. **Set C — Produce (3–5 items).** Translate or write your own sentences.
7. **Exit.** A confidence score from 1 to 5.

**Set D** is a spare set on the same rule. It runs only if a set scored 3/5 or lower, so a shaky rule gets five more goes before the next lesson.

**Mastery gate:** at most one item wrong in Set B and at most one in Set C before moving on (4/5, or 2/3 when a set has three items). Checkpoints (Lessons 9 and 17) need 80% overall.

**Feedback** comes in one table per set:

| # | What you wrote | Feedback | Correct version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | *Ar d'ól tú?* | 🔴 The *d'* drops after *ar* | *Ar ól tú?* |
| 2 | *Níor fhág sé.* | 🟢 🟣 séimhiú from *níor* | *Níor fhág sé.* |
| 3 | *Dfhág mé.* | 🟠 Missing apostrophe | *D'fhág mé.* |

🟢 correct · 🔴 error · 🔵 mutation error · 🟠 typo or missing fada · 🟣 pattern to notice

**Item rules:** every item has a number so you can answer by number. Brackets give the root and the person, e.g. *(cuir, mé)*. Unusual words are glossed after the item; the answer is never in the question.

### Key terms

The same terms are used in all three tense courses (past, present and future).

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Root (*fréamh*) | The verb's dictionary form, which is also the order to one person | *cuir, glan, ól* |
| Broad / slender (*leathan / caol*) | A root whose last vowel is *a, o, u* is broad; *e, i* is slender. It decides how an ending is spelt | *glan* (broad), *cuir* (slender) |
| Séimhiú (lenition) | An *h* after the first consonant | *cuir → chuir* |
| Urú (eclipsis) | A letter in front of the first consonant, or *n-* before a vowel | *an ndeachaigh, go n-ólann* |
| Particle | A small word in front of the verb that asks, denies or links | *ar, níor, gur, nár, an, ní, go, nach* |
| Statement form | The form used when you simply tell | *chuaigh, rinne, chonaic* |
| Dependent form | The second form some irregular verbs use after a particle; also called the dependent stem | *an ndeachaigh, ní dhearna, an bhfaca* |
| One-word form | A form with the person built into the ending | *chaitheamar, thángamar* |
| Impersonal form (*briathar saor*) | Says something was done without saying who did it | *rugadh, cailleadh* |
| Group A / Group B | This course's names for the irregular verbs that take *an / ní* (A) and *ar / níor* (B) | A: *téigh*; B: *tar* |

## Part A — Regular statements (Lessons 1–4)

Part A answers one question: what does the verb look like when you simply say what happened? The answer depends only on the root's first letter.

### Lesson 1 — Séimhiú ar chonsain

**Goal:** say the past of a regular verb that starts with a consonant.

**Starter** (no recall: this is where the past tense begins): read the model sentences aloud, then look at the first two letters of each bold verb. What do they all have in common?

**Model sentences** (Aoife and Máire, Aonad 5; Aonad 10):

- **Chaith** mé dhá mhí ag obair i gCaíteo, sin príomhchathair Eacuadór.
- Ar deireadh thiar, **thóg** mé bus an tslí ar fad ó Veiniséala síos go Peiriú.
- **Chaith** mé tráthnóna in Osu agus **bhuail** mé bóthar suas go Kumasi.
- **Shroich** mé an teach cónaithe tar éis ocht n-uair an chloig ar an mbóthar.
- **Cheannaigh** mé an léine seo nuair a bhí mé ar saoire sa Fhrainc agus **thaitin** an dath go mór liom.

*ar deireadh thiar* in the end · *bhuail mé bóthar* I hit the road · *teach cónaithe* residence

**The root** is the verb's dictionary form, which is also the order you give one person: *cuir!* (put!), *glan!* (clean!), *tóg!* (take!). Every past form in this course is built from the root.

**The rule:** b, c, d, g, m, p, s, t take an *h* after the first letter. The form is the same for every person: *chuir mé, chuir tú, chuir sé, chuir sí, chuir sibh, chuir siad* ("we" is Lesson 7).

| Root | Past | Root | Past |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cuir | chuir mé | bog | bhog mé |
| ceannaigh | cheannaigh mé | pós | phós mé |
| glan | ghlan mé | múch | mhúch mé |
| tóg | thóg mé | dún | dhún mé |
| seol | sheol mé | caith | chaith mé |

**Set A — Notice**

1–5. Find five past-tense verbs in the model sentences that follow this rule, and write each one's root.

**Set B — Apply** (write the full sentence in the past)

6. *(glan, mé)* an chistin aréir.
7. *(seol, sí)* téacs chuig a deartháir.
8. *(pós, siad)* i nGaillimh anuraidh.
9. *(múch, Peadar)* gach fearas san oíche. — *fearas* appliance
10. *(díol, sé)* a sheancharr. — *díol* sell

**Set C — Produce** (translate)

11. I bought a new jacket last week. — *seaicéad*
12. She moved to Cork two months ago. — *bog go*
13. They spent a week in Spain.

**Set D — Spare**

14. *(tosaigh, an rang)* ar a seacht.
15. *(buail, mé)* le Síle sa chathair.
16. *(cas, sé)* ar dheis ag na soilse. — *cas* turn
17. *(dún, sí)* an doras.
18. *(tiomáin, siad)* go Gaillimh.

**Watch for:** the *h* goes after the first letter only (*cheannaigh*, never *ceannhaigh*), and nothing is added to the end: the past is the bare root plus its *h* (*cheannaigh mé*, *chuir mé*).

### Lesson 2 — Litreacha nach n-athraíonn

**Goal:** know which consonants refuse the *h*.

**Recall:** R1. *(cuir, mé)* · R2. *(tóg, sé)* · R3. *(seol, siad)*

**Model sentences** (Melanie, Mary and Séamas, Aonad 11; Aoife, Aonad 5; Aonad 6):

- Thit pota a bhí lán d'uisce beirithe anuas ón sorn ar mo chos. Chuala m'fhear céile mé agus **rith** sé isteach chugam.
- **Leon** mé mo mhurnán cúpla bliain ó shin.
- **Léigh** mé píosa mar gheall ar na hAstacaigh i Meicsiceo.
- **Réitigh** gach duine go maith le chéile.
- Tá aithne ag pobal na Gaeilge ar Dermot Somers mar gheall ar na trí leabhar Gaeilge a **scríobh** sé.
- **Shleamhnaigh** an carr ar fud na háite.

*murnán* ankle · *réitigh le* get on with · *sleamhnaigh* slide, skid

**The rule:**

| Root starts with | Past | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| l, n, r | no change | léigh mé · leon mé · nigh mé · rith mé · réitigh mé |
| sc, sm, sp, st | no change | scríobh mé · smaoinigh mé · spreag mé · stop mé |
| s + vowel, l, n or r | séimhiú as normal | sheol mé · shiúil mé · shroich mé · shleamhnaigh mé |

Say it to see why: *shtop* and *shcríobh* can't be pronounced, so Irish leaves them alone.

Memory line: **Lazy Nuns Rarely Stop to Smile, or Speak about iScream**. The letters l, n, r, st, sm, sp and sc never take séimhiú.

4. sábháil

### Lesson 3 — D' roimh ghuta

**Goal:** say the past of a regular verb that starts with a vowel.

**Recall:** R1. *(rith, mé)* · R2. *(scríobh, sé)* · R3. *(sábháil, sí)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 4, 8 and 11):

- Sa chéad jab sealadach a fuair mé i mBéal Feirste, **d'oibrigh** mé mar fháilteoir.
- Bhí mé ag obair le comhlacht eile ach **d'éirigh** mé as dhá mhí ó shin.
- An mhaidin eile, **d'imigh** mo charr go huile agus go hiomlán as smacht orm.
- **D'ordaigh** mé an bradán agus bhí sé an-úr agus an-bhlasta.
- **D'oscail** cuid acu bialanna.

*sealadach* temporary · *éirigh as* resign · *as smacht* out of control

**The rule:** a vowel can't take an *h*, so the past adds *d'* in front instead. It is the same for every person.

| Root | Past | Root | Past |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ól | d'ól mé | aistrigh | d'aistrigh mé |
| éirigh | d'éirigh mé | inis | d'inis mé |
| imir | d'imir mé | ordaigh | d'ordaigh mé |
| oscail | d'oscail mé | ullmhaigh | d'ullmhaigh mé |

2. iarr *(sé)* — *ask for*
3. éist *(siad)*
4. athraigh *(sí)* — *change*
5. oscail *(tú)*

**Set B — Apply**

6. *(imir, mé)* leadóg le Clár inné.
7. *(inis, sí)* an scéal dom.
8. *(ullmhaigh, Peadar)* an dinnéar.
9. *(aistrigh, siad)* go Luimneach. — *aistrigh* move

**Set C — Produce**

12. He opened the office at nine.
13. She resigned from her job last year.

**Set D — Spare**

14. *(aimsigh, mé)* an t-óstán gan stró. — *aimsigh* find; *gan stró* easily
15. *(íoc, siad)* an bille. — *íoc* pay
16. *(ardaigh, sí)* a lámh.
17. *(eitil, sé)* go Páras. — *eitil* fly
18. *(úsáid, tú)* an ríomhaire.

**Watch for:** the apostrophe (*d'ól*, not *dól*), and no *h* on the vowel.

### Lesson 4 — D'fh, agus na ceithre riail

**Goal:** handle verbs in *f*, then choose the right rule for any regular verb.

**Recall:** R1. *(ól, mé)* · R2. *(rith, sí)* · R3. *(glan, siad)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 5, 6 and 7):

- **D'fhreastail** mé ar mheánscoil cailíní i gCathair na Gaillimhe.
- **D'fhág** mé Eacuadór agus chuaigh mé suas isteach sa Cholóim.
- **D'fhan** muid i bpuball.
- Ar fhéach tú ar an teilifís aréir? **D'fhéach.**
- Ar fhoghlaim tú an teanga? **D'fhoghlaim.**

*freastail ar* attend · *Ar … ?* Did … ? (questions are Lesson 5; just read them here)

**The rule:** *f* takes an *h* like any consonant, but *fh* is silent. The verb now starts with a vowel sound, so it also takes *d'*: *fág → d'fhág, fan → d'fhan, freagair → d'fhreagair, foghlaim → d'fhoghlaim, féach → d'fhéach, fill → d'fhill*.

**The four rules, checked in this order:**

- Starts with *f*? → *d'fh* (Lesson 4)
- Starts with a vowel? → *d'* (Lesson 3)
- Starts with l, n, r, sc, sm, sp or st? → no change (Lesson 2)
- Any other consonant → add *h* (Lesson 1)

3. *(fill, siad)* abhaile. — *fill* return
4. *(féach, sé)* ar an nuacht.
5. *(foghlaim, tú)* Spáinnis.

**Set B — Apply** (all four rules, mixed)

**Watch for:** *d'fh* needs both parts. *Fhág mé* and *D'fág mé* are both wrong.

## Part B — Regular verbs: ask, deny, "we", "that" (Lessons 5–9)

Part B adds the little words in front of the verb. For regular verbs every one of them ends in *-r* and every one lenites, which makes Part B one pattern used six ways.

### Lesson 5 — Ar / níor le consain

**Goal:** ask and say "no" with consonant verbs.

**Recall:** R1. *(fág, mé)* · R2. *(scríobh, sí)* · R3. *(éirigh, sé)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 5, 10 and 11):

- **Níor thuig** mé céard a bhí ag tarlú.

*gníomhaire taistil* travel agent · *aisteoireacht* acting

|  | Form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |

| No | *Níor* + séimhiú, verb alone | *Níor chuir.* |

### Lesson 6 — Imíonn an d'

**Goal:** ask and say "no" with vowel and *f* verbs, then with any regular verb.

**Recall:** R1. Question, yes and no for *(cuir, tú)* · R2. Make negative: *Léigh mé é.* · R3. *(fan, mé)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 5, 6 and 7):

| Root | Question | Yes | No |

### Lesson 7 — "We" san aimsir chaite

**Goal:** say "we did" two ways, and recognise the one-word forms when you hear them.

**Recall:** R1. Question, yes and no for *(fág, tú)* · R2. Make negative: *D'ól mé é.* · R3. *(léigh, sí)*

**Model sentences** (syllabus Topic 5; Aonaid 3, 8 and 11):

- **Chaitheamar** dhá lá ag sciáil.
- **Thógamar** an bád farantóireachta ón Aithin go Santorini.
- **Bhogamar** isteach sa chomharsanacht trí bliana ó shin.
- Chuamar go dtí an cósta agus **d'fhanamar** in óstán don oíche.
- **Chuireamar** uisce fuar ar mo chos.
- **Bhaineamar** an-taitneamh as an gcomhluadar.
- Faoin mbliain 1988, **thosaíomar** ag gabháil níos faide i gcéin.

*bád farantóireachta* ferry · *comhluadar* company · *i gcéin* far away · *chuamar* we went (Lesson 13)

**The rule:** two forms are correct. Verb + *muid* is always safe: *chaith muid, d'fhan muid, cheannaigh muid*. The one-word form is common in writing and in the listening tracks:

| Root type | Ending | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 syllable, last vowel a, o, u | *-amar* | *thógamar · d'fhanamar · d'ólamar · bhogamar* |
| 1 syllable, last vowel e, i | *-eamar* | *chaitheamar · chuireamar · bhaineamar · shroicheamar* |
| ends in *-(a)igh* | *-aíomar / -íomar* | *cheannaíomar · thosaíomar · d'éiríomar · bhailíomar* |
| *imir, oscail, inis* (shortened) | *-íomar / -aíomar* | *d'imríomar · d'osclaíomar · d'insíomar* |

Questions and negatives use the same words: *Ar chaitheamar? · Níor fhanamar.*

**Recognise only:** Munster speakers use *-adar / -eadar* for "they": *Chaitheadar go holc linn. Thógadar na horduithe míchearta.* (Aonad 8) = *chaith siad, thóg siad*.

**Goal:** report and give opinions about the past with regular verbs.

**Recall:** R1. *(caith, muid)*, one word · R2. *(ceannaigh, muid)*, one word · R3. Question, yes and no for *(ól, tú)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 5, 6, 10 and 11; syllabus):

**The rule:** every past particle for regular verbs ends in *-r*, lenites, and pushes out the *d'*.

| Particle | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Part C — Irregular verbs (Lessons 10–14)

### Lesson 10 — Grúpa B: cosúil leis na briathra rialta

**Goal:** use *beir, clois, ith, tabhair, tar* with everything from Part B.

**Recall:** R1. Question, yes and no for *(fág, tú)* · R2. *(ól, muid)*, one word · R3. Report: "Níor ól mé é." — *Dúirt sé …*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 2, 5 and 6):

### Lesson 11 — Grúpa A: fréamh nua

**Goal:** ask and deny with *téigh, déan, feic*, which switch to a second stem.

**Recall:** R1. Question, yes and no for *(tar, tú)* · R2. *(tabhair, mé)* · R3. Report: "Chuala mé é." — *Dúirt sé …*

**Model sentences** (Aoife, Aonad 5; Aonad 11):

- Nuair a bhí mé timpeall deich, **chonaic** mé alt an-suimiúil sa National Geographic.
- Nuair a **chuaigh** mé go dtí an ollscoil, **rinne** mé an cinneadh go ndéanfainn an Spáinnis.
- **An ndeachaigh** tú ar saoire anuraidh? **Ní dheachaigh.**
- **An ndearna** tú iarracht an teanga a labhairt? **Rinne.**
- **An bhfaca** tú Síofra ó tháinig sí abhaile? **Ní fhaca.**
- Bhí slua ann, daoine **nach bhfaca** mé le blianta fada.

*go ndéanfainn* that I would do (conditional; ignore for now) · *slua* crowd

**The rule:** each verb has a statement stem and a dependent stem. The dependent stem follows *an, ní, go, nach, cá* and *mura*. *An* adds urú; *ní* adds séimhiú. These verbs never take *ar* or *níor*.

| Root | Statement | Dependent stem | Question | No |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| téigh (go) | chuaigh | deachaigh | *An ndeachaigh tú?* | *Ní dheachaigh.* |
| déan (do, make) | rinne | dearna | *An ndearna tú?* | *Ní dhearna.* |
| feic (see) | chonaic | faca | *An bhfaca tú?* | *Ní fhaca.* |

The statement stem tells; the dependent stem asks and denies.

### Lesson 14 — Go / nach agus gur / nár

**Goal:** report and give opinions with every past verb.

### Lesson 15 — Focail cheiste san aimsir chaite

**Goal:** ask what, who, when, where and why about the past.

**Recall:** R1. Report: "Chuaigh mé abhaile." — *Dúirt Seán …* · R2. Report: "Níor tháinig mé." — *Dúirt Áine …* · R3. Question, yes and no for *(feic, tú)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 5, 8 and 10; syllabus):

- **Cén fáth ar roghnaigh** sibh an bhialann sin ar deireadh?
- **Cad a rinne** sibh tar éis na bialainne? **Cad a tharla?**
- Cuir síos ar **cá bhfuair** tú na héadaí.
- **Cad é mar a d'éirigh** leat ag an agallamh?

**The rule:** question words split into two groups.

| Question word | Then | Regular + Group B | Group A |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Cad a, Cé a, Cathain a, Cén uair a, Conas a, Cé mhéad a* | the plain statement form | *Cad a cheannaigh tú? · Cé a d'ól an bainne? · Cathain a tháinig sí?* | *Cad a rinne tú? · Cad a dúirt sí? · Cé a chonaic é?* |
| *Cá* (where) | the dependent form | *Cár* + séimhiú: *Cár fhan tú? · Cár chaith tú an samhradh?* | *Cá* + urú: *Cá ndeachaigh tú? · Cá raibh tú? · Cá bhfuair tú é?* |
| *Cén fáth, Cén áit* | the dependent form | *ar* + séimhiú: *Cén fáth ar fhág tú? · Cén áit ar fhan sibh?* | *a* + urú: *Cén fáth a ndeachaigh tú? · Cén fáth a raibh sé déanach?* |

Shortcut: *what, who, when, how* take the form you'd use in a statement. *Where* and *why* take the form you'd use after *ar* or *an*.

### Lesson 16 — An briathar saor

**Goal:** recognise the impersonal past in reading and listening, and use the common ones.

**Recall:** R1. Where did you go? — *(téigh, tú)* · R2. Why did he leave? — *(fág, sé)* · R3. What did she say? — *(abair, sí)*

**Model sentences** (Aonaid 2, 5, 11 and 14; syllabus):

- **Rugadh** buachaill beag do Shorcha.
- **Tógadh** i gContae Lú iad.
- Faraor, **cailleadh** mo mhála idir Baile Átha Cliath agus Accra.
- **Stopadh** an bus agus bhí saighdiúirí amuigh.
- Cé gur **baineadh** geit uafásach asam, **níor gortaíodh** mé ar chor ar bith.
- **Briseadh** as mo phost mé le gairid.
- Níor **craoladh** ach aon chlár amháin de *Saol Ella* go fóill.

*tógadh* were raised · *baineadh geit asam* I got a fright · *craoladh* was broadcast

**The rule:** the impersonal past says something was done without saying who did it.

| Root type | Ending | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 syllable, last vowel a, o, u | *-adh* | *tógadh · stopadh · pósadh · fágadh · óladh* |
| 1 syllable, last vowel e, i | *-eadh* | *cailleadh · briseadh · baineadh · cuireadh* |
| *-(a)igh* and other 2-syllable roots | *-aíodh / -íodh* | *gortaíodh · c**eannaíodh · bailíodh · osclaíodh* |

Three differences from the normal past: no séimhiú (*tógadh*, not *thógadh*); no *d'* (*óladh, fágadh*); *ar* and *níor* don't lenite it (*níor gortaíodh, ar rugadh?*). The person comes after: *Rugadh mé …, Briseadh as mo phost mé.*

Irregular, recognise only: *rugadh* (beir) · *rinneadh* (déan) · *tugadh* (tabhair) · *dúradh* (abair) · *itheadh* (ith) · *fuarthas* (faigh).

**Set A — Notice** (translate into English)

**Set B — Apply** (make it impersonal)

**Watch for:** *Níor ghortaíodh* is wrong. The impersonal never lenites.
