## Course overview

Thirteen lessons of 5–10 minutes each take you from the first future ending on a regular verb to making plans with *nuair a* and *má*, asking about the future and reading official announcements. Each lesson teaches one rule that B1 learners get wrong if it isn't drilled on its own, shows it in model sentences from the SNAS.ie TEG B1 learning materials, then drills it in sets of five until it is automatic.

The whole future tense rests on two ideas:

1. **One ending per verb type, for every person.** One-syllable verbs take *-faidh / -fidh*: *glanfaidh, cuirfidh*. Two-syllable verbs take *-óidh / -eoidh*: *ceannóidh, éireoidh*. The same form serves *mé, tú, sé, sí, sibh* and *siad* (*glanfaidh mé*); only "we" has its own ending (*glanfaimid*). The start of the verb never changes in a statement.
2. **The little words follow the usual rules, with three exceptions.** *An* + urú asks, *ní* + séimhiú says no, *go* and *nach* + urú report. Only *rachaidh* (go), *gheobhaidh* (get) and *déarfaidh* (say) break those rules.

|  | Little word | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question | *an* + urú | *An gcuirfidh tú?* · *An ólfaidh tú?* |
| No | *ní* + séimhiú | *Ní chuirfidh.* · *Ní ólfaidh.* |
| That | *go* + urú | *Sílim go gcuirfidh sé…* · *go n-ólfaidh* |
| That… not · Won't…? | *nach* + urú | *nach gcuirfidh* · *Nach mbeidh tú ann?* |
| Where? | *cá* + urú | *Cá bhfanfaidh tú?* |
| What, who, when, how? | *a* + séimhiú | *Cad a dhéanfaidh tú?* · *Cén t-am a bhuailfidh mé leat?* |
| When (in the future) | *nuair a* + séimhiú + future | *Nuair a bheidh tú críochnaithe…* |
| If | *má* + séimhiú + **present** | *Má bhíonn an t-am agam, rachaidh mé…* |
| If … not | *mura* + urú + **present** | *Mura ndéanann sé staidéar, teipfidh air.* |
| Before | *sula* + urú + future | *sula n-íosfaidh muid* |

## How every lesson runs

Every lesson has the same seven steps, and each step goes out as its own message so feedback comes after every five answers.

1. **Recall (1 min).** Three quick items from earlier lessons, so old rules stay warm. Lesson 1 has a short Starter instead, since nothing comes before it.
2. **Model sentences (1 min).** Short source sentences with the target forms in bold. Read them aloud once. Unconnected sentences are listed one by one; a connected story is set as a paragraph.
3. **The rule (1 min).** One small table. Nothing else is new.
4. **Set A — Notice (5 items).** Spot or build the form with the rule in front of you.
5. **Set B — Apply (5 items).** Use the rule in sentences, usually as question / yes / no.
6. **Set C — Produce (3–5 items).** Translate or write your own sentences.
7. **Exit.** A confidence score from 1 to 5.

**Set D** is a spare set on the same rule. It runs only if a set scored 3/5 or lower, so a shaky rule gets five more goes before the next lesson.

**Mastery gate:** at most one item wrong in Set B and at most one in Set C before moving on (4/5, or 2/3 when a set has three items). Checkpoints (Lessons 6 and 13) need 80% overall.

**Feedback** comes in one table per set:

| # | What you wrote | Feedback | Correct version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | *An n-ólfaidh tú tae?* | 🔵 *an* leaves a vowel alone | *An ólfaidh tú tae?* |
| 2 | *Ní chuirfidh.* | 🟢 🟣 séimhiú from *ní* | *Ní chuirfidh.* |
| 3 | *Má beidh an t-am agam…* | 🔴 *má* takes the present | *Má bhíonn an t-am agam…* |
| 4 | *Ceannoidh sí bainne.* | 🟠 Missing fada | *Ceannóidh sí bainne.* |

🟢 correct · 🔴 error · 🔵 mutation error · 🟠 typo or missing fada · 🟣 pattern to notice

**Item rules:** every item has a number so you can answer by number. Brackets give the root and the person, e.g. *(cuir, mé)*. Unusual words are glossed after the item; the answer is never in the question.

### Key terms

The same terms are used in all three tense courses (past, present and future).

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Root (*fréamh*) | The verb's dictionary form, which is also the order to one person | *cuir, glan, ól* |
| Broad / slender (*leathan / caol*) | A root whose last vowel is *a, o, u* is broad; *e, i* is slender. It decides how an ending is spelt | *glanfaidh* (broad), *cuirfidh* (slender) |
| Séimhiú (lenition) | An *h* after the first consonant | *ní chuirfidh* |
| Urú (eclipsis) | A letter in front of the first consonant, or *n-* before a vowel | *an gcuirfidh, go n-ólfaidh* |
| Particle | A small word in front of the verb that asks, denies or links | *an, ní, go, nach, cá* |
| Statement form | The form used when you simply tell | *rachaidh, gheobhaidh, beidh* |
| Dependent form | The second form some irregular verbs use after a particle | *an bhfaighidh, ní bhfaighidh* |
| One-word form | A form with the person built into the ending | *glanfaimid, cuirfimid* |
| Impersonal form (*briathar saor*) | Says something will be done without saying who will do it | *glanfar, déanfar* |

## Part A — Building the verb (Lessons 1–3)

Part A answers one question: what does the verb look like when you say what someone will do? The answer depends on the root's last vowel and its number of syllables.

### Lesson 1 — Briathra aonsiollacha

**Goal:** say what someone will do, with a one-syllable verb, in every person.

**Starter** (no recall: this is where the future tense begins): read the model sentences aloud, then look at the end of each bold verb. Which two endings do you see, and what decides between them?

**Model sentences** (Aonad 12; Aonad 15; syllabus; Caighdeán):

- **Cuirfidh** mé an litir sa phost. (Syl)
- **Fanfaidh** mé leat ag stad an bhus. (A12)
- **Buailfidh** mé leat ag an bpríomhdhoras. (A12)
- **Glaofaidh** mé ar ais i ndiaidh am lóin. (A12)
- Mura ndéanann sé aon staidéar, **teipfidh** air sa scrúdú. (A15)
- **Pósfaidh** siad an 26 Feabhra. (Caighdeán 1.1.5)

*stad an bhus* bus stop · *glaoigh* call · *teip ar* fail · *mura ndéanann sé* if he doesn't do (Lesson 11)

**The root** is the verb's dictionary form, which is also the order you give one person: *cuir!* (put!), *fan!* (wait!), *glan!* (clean!). Every future form in this course is built from the root.

**The rule:** look at the last vowel of the root. *a, o, u* (broad) → *-faidh*, and *-faimid* for "we". *e, i* (slender) → *-fidh*, and *-fimid* for "we". The same form serves *mé, tú, sé, sí, sibh* and *siad*: *glanfaidh mé, glanfaidh tú, glanfaidh siad*. For "we", verb + *muid* is equally correct: *glanfaimid* or *glanfaidh muid*. The start of the verb never changes in a statement.

| Root | mé, tú, sé, sí, sibh, siad | muid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| glan | glanfaidh | glanfaimid |
| fan | fanfaidh | fanfaimid |
| tóg | tógfaidh | tógfaimid |
| dún | dúnfaidh | dúnfaimid |
| cuir | cuirfidh | cuirfimid |
| buail | buailfidh | buailfimid |
| bris | brisfidh | brisfimid |

A root ending in *-bh, -mh* or *-th* keeps it before the *f*: *scríobhfaidh, caithfidh*.

**Set A — Notice**

1–5. Find five future verbs in the model sentences and write each one's root.

**Set B — Apply** (write the full sentence in the future)

6. *(glan, mé)* an chistin anocht.
7. *(fág, siad)* ar a hocht maidin amárach.
8. *(cuir, sí)* glaoch ar a máthair.
9. *(dún, muid)* an oifig go luath Dé hAoine. — one word
10. *(scríobh, sé)* ríomhphost chuig an mbainisteoir.

**Set C — Produce** (translate)

11. I'll wait for you at the station. — *fan le*
12. We'll close the shop at six. — one word
13. She'll meet Seán tomorrow. — *buail le*

**Set D — Spare**

14. *(bris, tú)* an cupán sin!
15. *(caith, muid)* an deireadh seachtaine i nGaillimh. — one word
16. *(seol, mé)* an litir amárach. — *seol* send
17. *(ól, siad)* cupán tae.
18. *(bain, sibh)* taitneamh as an scannán. — *bain taitneamh as* enjoy

**Watch for:** there is no one-word "I" form: *glanfaidh mé*, never *glanfaim*. And nothing goes on the front: *Glanfaidh mé*, never *Ghlanfaidh mé*.

### Lesson 2 — Briathra dhá shiolla

**Goal:** say the future of two-syllable verbs that end in *-aigh* or *-igh*.

**Recall:** R1. *(glan, mé)* · R2. *(cuir, muid)*, one word · R3. *(fan, siad)*

**Model sentences** (Aonad 12; Aonad 15):

- **Ceannóidh** mise deochanna agus briosca sa siopa. (A12)
- Go hiontach! **Imeoidh** muid. (A15)
- **Roghnóidh** Foireann A ceann ar bith de na boscaí. (A12)
- An **éireoidh** tú as na toitíní lá éigin? (A15)

*mise* I (emphatic) · *imigh* go off · *roghnaigh* choose · *éirigh as* give up

**The rule:** take off *-aigh* or *-igh*, then add the ending. *-aigh* → *-óidh*, and *-óimid* for "we". *-igh* → *-eoidh*, and *-eoimid* for "we".

| Root | mé, tú, sé, sí, sibh, siad | muid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ceannaigh | ceannóidh | ceannóimid |
| tosaigh | tosóidh | tosóimid |
| roghnaigh | roghnóidh | roghnóimid |
| éirigh | éireoidh | éireoimid |
| imigh | imeoidh | imeoimid |
| bailigh | baileoidh | baileoimid |

**Set A — Notice** (write the *sé* form)

2. éirigh
3. tosaigh
4. bailigh — *collect*
5. críochnaigh — *finish*

**Set B — Apply**

7. *(ullmhaigh, sí)* an dinnéar anocht. — *ullmhaigh* prepare
8. *(tosaigh, muid)* ag obair ar a naoi. — one word
9. *(ceannaigh, mé)* na ticéid ar líne.
10. *(imigh, siad)* abhaile ar a cúig.

**Set C — Produce** (translate)

11. I'll buy a present for her. — *bronntanas di*
12. The class will start at seven.
13. We'll collect the children from school. — one word

**Set D — Spare**

14. *(bailigh, mé)* na leanaí.
15. *(socraigh, sé)* an cruinniú. — *socraigh* arrange
16. *(athraigh, muid)* an plean. — *athraigh* change; one word
17. *(críochnaigh, mé)* an obair Dé hAoine.

**Watch for:** the *ó* carries a fada: *ceannóidh*, never *ceannoidh*. And *-igh* verbs keep the *e*: *éireoidh*, never *éiróidh*.

### Lesson 3 — Giorrú agus foirmeacha corra

**Goal:** handle verbs that shorten and verbs with odd stems, then choose the right ending for any regular verb.

**Recall:** R1. *(ceannaigh, mé)* · R2. *(éirigh, muid)*, one word · R3. *(glan, sí)*

**Model sentences** (Aonad 7; Aonad 12; Aonad 15; Caighdeán):

- An **imreoidh** tú cluiche leadóige liom? (A12)
- An **osclóidh** sé doras na hoifige dúinn? (A12)
- **Inseoidh** an aimsir. (Caighdeán 1.1.5)
- **Foghlaimeoidh** tú conas labhairt faoi do chaithimh aimsire féin. (A7)
- **Sábhálfaimid** airgead má thugaimid ár mbia féin linn. (A12)
- An **dtiomáinfidh** tú go Gaillimh anocht? (A15)

*Inseoidh an aimsir.* Time will tell. · *má thugaimid* if we bring (Lesson 11)

**The rule:** two-syllable verbs ending in *-il, -in, -ir* or *-is* lose the vowel of their last syllable, then take *-óidh / -eoidh*. A few don't shorten: *foghlaim → foghlaimeoidh*, *freastail → freastalóidh*, *taistil → taistealóidh*.

| Root | mé, tú, sé, sí, sibh, siad | muid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| imir | imreoidh | imreoimid |
| inis | inseoidh | inseoimid |
| oscail | osclóidh | osclóimid |
| labhair | labhróidh | labhróimid |
| freagair | freagróidh | freagróimid |
| codail | codlóidh | codlóimid |

**Odd stems that take the one-syllable endings:**

- *-áil* and *-áin* drop the *i*: *sábháil → sábhálfaidh*, *taispeáin → taispeánfaidh*, *marcáil → marcálfaidh*. *Tiomáin* stays slender: *tiomáinfidh*.
- One-syllable verbs in *-igh* drop the *-igh*: *léigh → léifidh*, *suigh → suífidh*, *nigh → nífidh*, *glaoigh → glaofaidh*.

**The five rules, checked in this order:**

- Two or more syllables, ends in *-(a)igh*? → *-óidh / -eoidh* (Lesson 2)
- Two syllables, ends in *-il, -in, -ir, -is*? → shorten, then *-óidh / -eoidh* (Lesson 3)
- Ends in *-áil* or *-áin*? → *-álfaidh, -ánfaidh* (Lesson 3)
- One syllable, broad? → *-faidh / -faimid* (Lesson 1)
- One syllable, slender? → *-fidh / -fimid* (Lesson 1)

| --- | --- | --- |

| f | *An bhfanfaidh tú?* | *Ní fhanfaidh.* |
| g | *An nglanfaidh tú?* | *Ní ghlanfaidh.* |
| p, t | *An bpósfaidh siad? · An dtógfaidh tú?* | *Ní phósfaidh. · Ní thógfaidh.* |
| vowel | *An ólfaidh tú?* (no change) | *Ní ólfaidh.* (no change) |
| m, s + vowel | *An múchfaidh tú? · An seolfaidh tú?* (no urú) | *Ní mhúchfaidh. · Ní sheolfaidh.* |
| l, n, r, sc, sm, sp, st | *An rithfidh tú?* (no urú) | *Ní rithfidh.* (no séimhiú) |

### Lesson 7 — Beidh

**Goal:** say what will be, and what you will be doing.

**Recall:** R1. *(ceannaigh, mé)* · R2. Question, yes and no for *(fan, tú)* · R3. Sílim \_\_\_ *(cuir)* sé glaoch orm.

### Lesson 11 — Nuair a, má agus mura

**Goal:** talk about when things will happen, and set conditions with "if".

**Recall:** R1. Cad a \_\_\_ *(déan)* tú? · R2. Cá \_\_\_ *(bí)* tú? · R3. Make negative: *Rachaidh mé.*

**Model sentences** (Aonad 12; Aonad 15; syllabus):

- An ndúnfaidh tú an ríomhaire síos **nuair a bheidh** tú críochnaithe? (A12)
- **Má bhíonn** an t-am agam, rachaidh mé go Béal Feirste. (Syl)
- **Má bhíonn** an aimsir go maith, rachaidh mé ag siúl. (A15)
- **Má thagann** tú go dtí na siopaí liom, ceannóidh mé milseáin duit. (A15)
- **Mura ndéanann** sé aon staidéar, teipfidh air sa scrúdú. (A15)
- An nglanfaidh tú an chistin **sula n-íosfaidh** muid? (A15)

*dún síos* shut down · *milseáin* sweets · *sula* before

**The rule:** *nuair a* (when) and *sula* (before) take the future. *Má* (if) and *mura* (if … not) take the **present**, even though the meaning is future. The result goes in the future.

| Word | Mutation | Tense | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *nuair a* | séimhiú | future | *Nuair a thiocfaidh tú, íosfaimid.* · *nuair a gheobhaidh mé é* |
| *sula* | urú | future | *sula mbeidh an trácht go dona* |
| *má* | séimhiú | present | *Má bhíonn an t-am agam…* · *Má théann tú…* |
| *má* + *tá, deir* | none | present | *Má tá tú tuirseach…* · *Má deir sí é…* |
| *mura* | urú | present | *Mura dtagann an bus…* · *Mura bhfuil / mbíonn an t-airgead agam…* |

Present forms you need here: *bíonn* (usually is), *téann* (goes), *tagann* (comes), *déanann* (does), *faigheann* (gets), *deir* (says), plus regular *-ann / -eann* and *-aíonn / -íonn* endings.

**Set A — Notice** (write the verb)

### Lesson 12 — An briathar saor
